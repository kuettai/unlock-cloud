🧩 Puzzle Selection — Group Quiz Mode

Source: docs/puzzle-taxonomy.json (74 puzzles, rarity/complexity/fun/interaction/duration/replay). Session: 3–5 players, 1 room, server pushes identical questions per voted category.

Constraint driving this shortlist: the puzzle must carry a plain question+answer payload the server can push (no card artwork / scenario-specific state). Green rows = puzzle natively models a question bank. Yellow row = best used as a group-consensus finale, not a question carrier itself.
Update: evidence-lock was dropped from the shortlist. pillar-lock was corrected twice — first to True/False sorting within a single category, then to a templated format: one authored fact per statement with an embedded '{correct|wrong}' slot, resolved randomly client-side (no separate true/false authoring needed, and no component fork required — pillar-lock.js is reused unmodified via a thin resolver function). streak-lock was dropped and replaced with spelling-lock — streak-lock and wager-lock felt too similar (both are MCQ-runner-with-scoring), and wire-lock/match-lock were considered but rejected since the trivia bank's questions/answers are mostly too long for their tight label layouts (only 62% of mcq answers are ≤20 chars, and 99% of questions exceed 25 chars). spelling-lock is a genuinely distinct mechanic from word-lock: word-lock uses per-position reels with random unrelated decoy letters (recall-and-select), while spelling-lock gives you the exact letters of the answer already shuffled and asks you to rearrange them (recall-and-order) — no typo risk, no decoy ambiguity, good fit for competitive scoring. Final shortlist: keypad-lock, word-lock, pillar-lock (templated True/False), spelling-lock, wager-lock.

Suggested 5-puzzle lineup

  1. keypad-lock (COMMON · complexity 1 · fun 1 · tap · quick) — opener, numeric-answer warmup
  2. word-lock (COMMON · complexity 1 · fun 2 · drag · quick) — short word/acronym answer, different interaction type
  3. pillar-lock (COMMON · complexity 2 · fun 2 · tap · medium) — categorization drill, breaks up the tap streak with a different rhythm
  4. spelling-lock (COMMON · complexity 2 · fun 2 · tap · medium) — rearrange shuffled letters (own answer's letters, no random decoys) — recall-and-order, distinct from word-lock's recall-and-select
  5. wager-lock (RARE · complexity 3 · fun 4 · tap · medium) — confidence-stake MCQ finale, satisfies "1+ RARE" guidance and ends on complexity/fun peak
Interaction spread: tap / drag / tap / tap / tap. Complexity arc: 1 → 1 → 2 → 2 → 3 — rises steadily, ends medium-high per taxonomy guidance.
Design guidance from the taxonomy applied:
✅ Includes 1 RARE (wager-lock) as the finale — satisfies "1+ RARE/LEGENDARY" rule.
✅ fun:4+ present once (wager-lock) — satisfies "1 fun:4+ per 3 rooms" easily since this is a single room.
✅ Complexity arc now rises cleanly (1→1→2→2→3) and ends medium-high, per guidance.
✅ No two puzzles share the same core scoring mechanic — dropped streak-lock specifically because it and wager-lock were both "MCQ pool + accumulate score to target," which felt redundant across two of five slots.
⚠️ Still 4 of 5 puzzles are tap interaction (only word-lock is drag) — considered wire-lock and match-lock for interaction variety, but rejected since the trivia bank's content (mostly full-sentence questions/answers) doesn't fit their tight label layouts without a separate short-label authoring pass.
