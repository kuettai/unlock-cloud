🧪 Puzzle Prototypes — New Mechanics (Claude v1)

Deduction | Push Luck | Decay | Fog Map | Crafting | Trap Disarm | Wager | Auction | Streak

🔍 Deduction Grid Lock

Logic grid elimination. Use clues to deduce who accessed what system, and when. Tap cells to cycle: empty → O (yes) → X (no).

🎲 Push-Your-Luck Lock

Each consecutive draw increases both your multiplier AND bust chance. Draw 1 = safe but 1×. Draw 4 = 4× but ~50% bust. Playing safe costs you rounds. Push too hard and lose everything.

💀 Decay Lock

Server logs are corrupting in real-time. Memorise the key information before it's gone, then answer from memory.

🗺️ Fog Map Lock

Explore the grid by revealing adjacent tiles. Collect 3 keycards before reaching the exit. Watch your energy — traps drain it, bonuses restore it. Choose your path wisely.

⚗️ Craft Lock

Combine materials based on their properties (tags), not fixed recipes. Select two items to combine. Items marked ∞ are tools — they aren't consumed. Discover the chain to produce the final item.

💣 Trap Disarm Lock

Cut wires in the correct order using a partially redacted manual. Some rules have blacked-out info — deduce the right sequence from what you CAN read. 3 strikes and the trap triggers.

🎰 Wager Lock — "Bluff the Guard"

You got caught near the vault. Talk your way out. Mumble = safe but only 2 choices (easy). Brazen Lie = high reward but 6 choices to pick from (hard). Reach enough trust to walk free.

🏷️ Auction Lock

Bid on mystery crates with limited budget. Some contain key items, some are decoys. Read the hints, decide how much to risk. Pass on junk, bid smart on essentials. Acquire 3 keys to unlock.

🔥 Streak Lock

Rapid-fire questions with a countdown timer. Consecutive correct answers build a combo multiplier (×1, ×2, ×3...). One wrong answer or timeout resets it to ×1. Race to the target score.
