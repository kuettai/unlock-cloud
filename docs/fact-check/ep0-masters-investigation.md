## Warnings (⚠️)

- ⚠️ **Fragment #4 minor wording variant**: Reuben says "Whatever he tells you, do it" while the WEB has "Whatever he says to you, do it." This is a natural paraphrase within dialogue — a character retelling what he heard. The fragment annotation itself uses the correct WEB wording. **No correction needed** — this is good storytelling.

- ⚠️ **Fragment #1 attribution**: In the Bible, the "ruler of the feast" speaks John 2:10 to the bridegroom. The narrative has Matthias (who IS the master/ruler of the feast) say this line. This is correct — the narrative simply gives the unnamed biblical "ruler of the feast" the fictional name Matthias. **No correction needed.**

- ⚠️ **Jesus' mother's name**: The narrative calls her "Miriam, from Nazareth." The Gospel of John never names Jesus' mother — she is only called "Jesus' mother" or "woman." The name Mary/Miriam comes from the Synoptic Gospels (Matthew 1:16, Luke 1:27, etc.). Using "Miriam" (the Hebrew form of Mary) is historically and theologically sound, but it is not from John's Gospel specifically. **Acceptable for the game context.**

| **Calculation** | 6 jars × 20-30 gallons = **120-180 gallons** | ✅ Matches |

The WEB itself uses the word "metretes" — confirmed correct. The narrative's volume calculation of 120-180 gallons is arithmetically correct based on the WEB footnote's per-jar estimate of 20-30 US gallons.

**Note**: Some scholarly sources give the metretes as closer to 8.75 gallons (Attic metretes = 39.39 liters ÷ 3.785 liters/gallon ≈ 10.4 gallons), which would yield 6 × (2-3 × 10.4) = 125-187 gallons. The WEB footnote's 20-30 gallons per jar and the narrative's 120-180 total are both reasonable approximations.
